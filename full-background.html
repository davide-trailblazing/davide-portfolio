How I coach founders · in the business

Ostrich: head down

I help founders win the next customer. First we learn by hand, then build repeatable sales with healthy margins.

1Customer Persona

Find the real problem.

Identify who needs the solution and why it matters.

Methods and exercises
  • Ideal customer profile (ICP) and day-in-the-life personas
  • Customer interviews using the Mom Test and Pain Funnel
  • Jobs-to-be-done framing
  • Riskiest-assumption testing and early-adopter identification
  • Problem-solution canvas
2Customer Buying Decision

Understand how they buy.

Find the decision-makers, buying criteria, and willingness to pay.

Methods and exercises
  • Buying-committee mapping
  • Sales qualification using MEDDPIC and SPIN
  • Willingness-to-pay tests
  • Return-on-investment calculators
  • Objection-handling practice
3Customer Journey

Make sales repeatable.

Build the path from first awareness to repeat business.

Methods and exercises
  • Customer-journey mapping, from awareness to expansion
  • Distribution channels and ecosystem building
  • Sales pipelines, demand staging, and conversion-rate reviews
  • Retention, expansion, and reasons for customer churn
4Customer Economics

Grow with healthy margins.

Connect pricing, acquisition costs, and customer lifetime value.

Methods and exercises
  • Business model and pricing
  • Customer lifetime value (LTV) and customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  • Upselling and cross-selling

These methods support individual founder coaching and the gener8tor seven-week sales challenge.

How I coach founders · on the business

Ostrich: head up

I help founders step back and steer the whole venture. We look for blindspots across four areas.

1Go-to-Market

Borrow what works.

Adapt proven approaches from other industries to your market.

Blindspot and coaching tools

Blindspot: Assuming your industry is too different to learn from others.

  • Compare commercial approaches and distribution models across industries
  • Test the approaches that fit the venture’s growth goals
2Funding & Financial Management

Fund the next milestone.

Connect cash flow and funding needs to what comparable companies have proved.

Blindspot and coaching tools

Blindspot: Choosing a funding target without evidence or milestones behind it.

  • Benchmark financing and milestones against comparable companies
  • Cash-flow management, runway, and milestones-to-funds plans
  • Equity, grants, state incentives, and strategic funding
  • Investor story: problem, market opening, why now, traction, and ask
  • Fundraising materials and data-room preparation
  • 60+ introductions per founder across mentors, investors, and ecosystem partners
3Human (Talent)

Give the right work to the right people.

Clarify ownership, find missing skills, and free the founder to lead.

Blindspot and coaching tools

Blindspot: The founder holding work the team should own.

  • Map responsibilities and the skill gap blocking growth
  • Decide what the founder keeps, delegates, or hires for
4Product (Tech)

Tie the build to customer value.

Set product milestones that demonstrate value and support revenue.

Blindspot and coaching tools

Blindspot: Building features without a clear path to customer value or revenue.

  • Connect product and technology milestones to commercial proof
  • Build, test, and learn from customer feedback
  • Minimum viable product (MVP) iteration
Turn business goals into weekly actions

Three inputs help us decide what matters this week.

  1. Business goalsObjectives, KPIs, 1–3 actions, and blockers for each focus area.
  2. Weekly progressWins, work in progress, and blockers. Review leading and lagging indicators against one North-Star metric.
  3. A founder conversation13 core questions about customers, revenue, team, runway, goals, and the biggest growth blocker.

Choose actions by impact and effort

P1 · Do nowHigh impact · Low effort
P2 · PlanHigh impact · High effort
P3 · If time allowsLow impact · Low effort
Park itLow impact · High effort

Do the P1s, record progress, and revisit the map each week.
